# Procedure Cortex — Integrated Experimental Capability (alpha.7.4)

Procedure Cortex is now integrated behind ASMEngine APIs, but remains **experimental in research status**: its outputs do not bypass ASM evidence authority and promotion claims still require fair benchmarks.

## Current capabilities

- safe typed executable DSL;
- autonomous unary integer procedure induction;
- bounded 2–4 input affine/compositional integer induction;
- Boolean procedure induction;
- conservative piecewise-affine conditional induction;
- held-out validation and versioned procedure history;
- contradiction-driven abstention/suspension;
- source-regime dependencies;
- evidence/provenance roots and duplicate-root protection;
- structural donor reuse without trust inheritance;
- read-only disagreement-driven information queries;
- drift revision and full-reversal revalidation;
- SQLite persistence and snapshot migration.

## Authority boundary

Procedure hypotheses, donor structures, and information queries are proposals. They do not directly become belief or action. Externally resolved evidence, source trust/regimes, independent roots, and held-out validation remain required. Information queries never fabricate outcomes or self-train from simulated answers.

## Current limitations

- no arbitrary code generation;
- no Python `eval`;
- no general learned search grammar;
- no autonomous execution of real-world experiments;
- no broad latent-variable discovery;
- conditional search is deliberately shallow/bounded;
- current information gathering selects a discriminating input, not a long-horizon experiment policy;
- contextual routing among many simultaneously valid procedures remains a next-stage problem.
